# Nutrition — Topic 2: Micronutrients & Vitamins

## What Are Micronutrients?

Your body also needs small things from food. We call them **micronutrients**. They are:

- Vitamins
- Minerals

You need only a small amount. But they are very important. They help your body work well every day.

## Important Vitamins

Here are four important vitamins:

- **Vitamin A** — good for your eyes and your skin
- **Vitamin C** — helps your body heal and fight sickness
- **Vitamin D** — helps your bones stay strong
- **Vitamin B12** — helps your blood and your brain

## Important Minerals

Here are two important minerals:

- **Iron** — carries oxygen in your blood
- **Calcium** — builds strong bones and teeth

## Common Deficiencies

A **deficiency** happens when your body does not get enough of a vitamin or mineral.

- Low iron can make you feel tired and weak. This is called **anemia**.
- Low vitamin D can make your bones weak.
- Low vitamin B12 can also make you feel tired.
- Low calcium can make your bones and teeth weak.

Many people do not get enough of these. It is good to check your food.

## Foods That Cover the Gaps

| Nutrient | Good Food Sources |
|---|---|
| Vitamin A | Carrots, sweet potato, spinach |
| Vitamin C | Orange, lemon, bell pepper |
| Vitamin D | Salmon, egg yolk, sunlight |
| Vitamin B12 | Meat, fish, eggs, milk |
| Iron | Red meat, beans, spinach |
| Calcium | Milk, cheese, yogurt, broccoli |

## Key Words

- **Micronutrient** — a small but important part of food (vitamins, minerals)
- **Vitamin** — a nutrient that helps your body work well
- **Mineral** — a nutrient from the earth that your body needs
- **Deficiency** — not enough of a vitamin or mineral
- **Anemia** — a problem from not enough iron
