# Nutrition — Topic 1: Macronutrients Basics (Carbs, Protein, Fat)

## What Are Macronutrients?

Your body needs food every day. Food has three main parts. We call them **macronutrients**. They are:

- Carbohydrates (carbs)
- Protein
- Fat

Your body uses all three parts. Each part has a different job.

## Carbohydrates

Carbs give your body **energy**. Energy helps you walk, think, and work.

Good carbs are in:

- Bread
- Rice
- Pasta
- Fruit
- Vegetables

Eat whole grains more than white bread or white rice. Whole grains have more **fiber**. Fiber is good for your stomach.

## Protein

Protein helps your body **grow**. It also helps your muscles get strong.

Good protein is in:

- Chicken
- Fish
- Eggs
- Beans
- Milk and cheese

Eat some protein every day. Your body needs it to repair itself.

## Fat

Fat is not bad. Your body needs some fat. Fat helps your body use vitamins.

Good fat is in:

- Olive oil
- Nuts
- Avocado
- Fish like salmon

Eat less fat from fried food and fast food. This fat is not healthy.

## How Much Do You Need?

You don't need to count every number. Use a simple rule: the **plate method**.

Look at your plate. Divide it into parts:

- Half the plate: vegetables and fruit
- One quarter: protein
- One quarter: carbs (like rice or potato)
- A little fat (like olive oil) on top

This is an easy way to eat well every day.

## Practice: Build Your Balanced Plate

Now it's your turn. Look at your next meal. Use the plate method.

**Step 1:** Draw a circle. This is your plate.

**Step 2:** Divide the circle:

- Half for vegetables and fruit
- One quarter for protein
- One quarter for carbs

**Step 3:** Write one food for each part. Example:

| Part of the Plate | Food Example |
|---|---|
| Vegetables and fruit | Tomato, carrot, apple |
| Protein | Grilled chicken |
| Carbs | Brown rice |
| Fat | Olive oil |

### Sample Meal (Simple Recipe)

**Chicken and Rice Plate**

Ingredients:

- 1 chicken breast
- 1 cup of brown rice
- 1 cup of mixed vegetables (carrot, broccoli)
- 1 spoon of olive oil
- Salt and pepper

Steps:

1. Cook the rice in water for 20 minutes.
2. Cook the chicken in a pan for 10 minutes. Turn it one time.
3. Cook the vegetables in a pan for 5 minutes.
4. Put the rice, chicken, and vegetables on your plate.
5. Add the olive oil, salt, and pepper on top.

Enjoy your balanced meal!

## Key Words

- **Macronutrient** — a big part of food (carbs, protein, fat)
- **Carbohydrate** — gives energy
- **Protein** — helps muscles grow
- **Fat** — helps the body use vitamins
- **Plate method** — a simple way to balance a meal
