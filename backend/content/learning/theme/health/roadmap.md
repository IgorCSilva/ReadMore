# Learning Roadmap — Theme: Health

This is the table of contents for the "Good Health" learning theme. It lists **chapters** and **topics** only — no full content yet. Content for a specific topic is generated on request, referencing that topic's ID below, and must always include something to *do in practice* (a recipe, an exercise routine, a habit/practice checklist, etc.), not just theory.

## 1. Chapter Map

| Chapter ID | Title | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| NUT | Nutrition | Understand what the body needs from food and build sustainable, practical eating habits. |
| EXE | Physical Exercise | Build a sustainable movement routine covering cardio, strength, and mobility. |
| SLP | Sleep | Understand sleep needs and build habits that improve sleep quality. |
| MEN | Mental Health Care | Build practical habits for managing stress, emotions, and overall mental well-being. |

## 2. Topics per Chapter

### NUT — Nutrition

1. **NUT-1 — Macronutrients Basics (Carbs, Protein, Fat).** What each macronutrient does, how much you roughly need, and how to balance a plate. *Practice angle: build a balanced-plate template + one sample meal.*
2. **NUT-2 — Micronutrients & Vitamins.** Key vitamins/minerals, common deficiencies, and which foods cover them. *Practice angle: a shopping list covering the most common gaps.*
3. **NUT-3 — Hydration.** How much water you actually need, signs of dehydration, hydration beyond plain water. *Practice angle: a daily hydration habit/checklist.*
4. **NUT-4 — Meal Planning & Portion Control.** Planning meals ahead, reading hunger/fullness cues, portion-size reference tricks. *Practice angle: a simple weekly meal-planning template.*
5. **NUT-5 — Reading Food Labels.** Understanding nutrition labels and ingredient lists to spot hidden sugar/sodium/fat. *Practice angle: a label-reading checklist to use while shopping.*
6. **NUT-6 — Reducing Sugar & Processed Foods.** Why ultra-processed food and added sugar matter, and practical swaps. *Practice angle: 5 easy whole-food swaps for common processed snacks.*
7. **NUT-7 — Healthy Eating on a Budget.** Eating well without overspending — bulk staples, seasonal produce, batch cooking. *Practice angle: a low-cost healthy grocery list + one batch-cook recipe.*
8. **NUT-8 — Mindful Eating.** Eating slowly, without distraction, tuning into real hunger vs. emotional/boredom eating. *Practice angle: a mindful-eating practice to try at the next meal.*

### EXE — Physical Exercise

1. **EXE-1 — Benefits of Exercise & Getting Started.** Why regular movement matters, overcoming the "getting started" barrier, setting realistic goals. *Practice angle: a first-week starter routine for a beginner.*
2. **EXE-2 — Cardio Fundamentals.** Types of cardio, target intensity, how much is enough. *Practice angle: a 20–30 minute beginner cardio session (walk/run/bike).*
3. **EXE-3 — Strength Training Basics.** Why strength training matters at any age, basic movement patterns (push, pull, squat, hinge). *Practice angle: a bodyweight or light-dumbbell full-body routine.*
4. **EXE-4 — Flexibility & Mobility.** Why mobility work prevents injury and improves daily movement. *Practice angle: a 10-minute daily stretching/mobility routine.*
5. **EXE-5 — Building a Sustainable Routine.** Scheduling workouts, combining cardio/strength/mobility across a week, avoiding burnout. *Practice angle: a sample weekly workout schedule.*
6. **EXE-6 — Exercise for Desk Workers & Sedentary Lifestyles.** Counteracting long sitting hours — posture, micro-breaks, desk-friendly exercises. *Practice angle: a set of desk/office-friendly stretches and movement breaks.*
7. **EXE-7 — Recovery & Injury Prevention.** Rest days, warm-up/cool-down, recognizing overtraining and early injury signs. *Practice angle: a warm-up/cool-down routine template.*
8. **EXE-8 — Tracking Progress & Staying Motivated.** Ways to measure progress beyond the scale, habit-tracking, staying consistent. *Practice angle: a simple weekly progress-tracking habit.*

### SLP — Sleep

1. **SLP-1 — Sleep Fundamentals.** Sleep cycles/stages, how much sleep is actually needed by age/lifestyle. *Practice angle: a quick self-check to estimate your personal sleep need.*
2. **SLP-2 — Sleep Hygiene Basics.** Core habits that support good sleep: consistent schedule, room environment, caffeine/alcohol timing. *Practice angle: a sleep-hygiene checklist.*
3. **SLP-3 — Building a Bedtime Routine.** Designing a wind-down routine that signals the body it's time to sleep. *Practice angle: a sample 30–45 minute wind-down routine.*
4. **SLP-4 — Managing Screen Time & Light Exposure.** Blue light, screens before bed, morning light exposure for circadian rhythm. *Practice angle: a practical screen-cutoff/light-exposure habit.*
5. **SLP-5 — Dealing with Insomnia & Stress-Related Sleep Issues.** Common causes of poor sleep and non-medication coping techniques. *Practice angle: a relaxation technique to use when unable to sleep.*
6. **SLP-6 — Naps & Sleep Scheduling.** When naps help vs. hurt, scheduling sleep around shift work/travel. *Practice angle: guidelines for an effective short nap.*
7. **SLP-7 — Nutrition & Sleep Connection.** How food/drink timing and choices affect sleep quality. *Practice angle: a list of sleep-friendly vs. sleep-disrupting foods/drinks and timing tips.*

### MEN — Mental Health Care

1. **MEN-1 — Stress Management Basics.** What stress does to the body/mind, healthy vs. unhealthy coping patterns. *Practice angle: a quick stress-relief technique (e.g., breathing exercise).*
2. **MEN-2 — Mindfulness & Meditation.** What mindfulness is, common meditation styles, realistic ways to start. *Practice angle: a beginner-friendly guided meditation practice.*
3. **MEN-3 — Building Healthy Habits & Routines.** How habits form, habit stacking, making routines stick. *Practice angle: a simple habit-building plan for one target habit.*
4. **MEN-4 — Managing Anxiety.** Recognizing anxiety patterns and practical grounding/coping techniques. *Practice angle: a grounding exercise to use in the moment.*
5. **MEN-5 — Social Connection & Support Systems.** Why relationships matter for mental health, maintaining and building connection. *Practice angle: a small, concrete action to strengthen one relationship this week.*
6. **MEN-6 — Work-Life Balance.** Setting boundaries, avoiding burnout, protecting personal time. *Practice angle: a boundary-setting exercise or weekly schedule rebalance.*
7. **MEN-7 — Journaling & Self-Reflection.** Using journaling to process emotions and track mental state over time. *Practice angle: a simple journaling prompt set to start with.*
8. **MEN-8 — Recognizing When to Seek Professional Help.** Signs that self-care isn't enough, how to find and approach professional support. *Practice angle: a checklist of warning signs + how to take the first step.*
