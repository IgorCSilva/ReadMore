# Nutrition — Topic 3: Hydration

## What Is Hydration?

Your body needs water every day. Water is in every part of your body. We call this **hydration**.

Water helps your body:

- Carry nutrients
- Control your temperature
- Clean waste from your body
- Keep your joints working well

## How Much Water Do You Need?

You don't need to count every drop. Use a simple rule: drink about **8 glasses of water** every day.

You need more water when:

- You exercise
- It is hot outside
- You feel sick

Check your urine. Light yellow is good. Dark yellow means you need more water.

## Signs of Dehydration

**Dehydration** happens when your body does not have enough water.

Signs of dehydration are:

- You feel thirsty
- Your mouth feels dry
- Your urine is dark
- You feel tired
- You have a headache
- You feel dizzy

If you see these signs, drink water right away.

## Hydration Beyond Water

Water is not the only way to hydrate. Many foods and drinks have water too.

Good sources of hydration are:

- Fruit, like watermelon and orange
- Vegetables, like cucumber and lettuce
- Soup
- Milk
- Tea without sugar

Drink less soda and sugary juice. They do not hydrate your body well.

## Practice: Build Your Daily Hydration Habit

Now it's your turn. Build a simple hydration habit for your day.

**Step 1:** Get a water bottle. Fill it with water.

**Step 2:** Drink water at these times:

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| When you wake up | Drink 1 glass of water |
| Before each meal | Drink 1 glass of water |
| During exercise | Drink small sips of water |
| Before bed | Drink 1 glass of water |

**Step 3:** Make a checklist for one day. Check each box when you drink a glass of water.

- [ ] Glass 1 — morning
- [ ] Glass 2 — before lunch
- [ ] Glass 3 — afternoon
- [ ] Glass 4 — before dinner
- [ ] Glass 5 — evening

**Step 4:** Check your urine color at the end of the day. Light yellow means you did well.

## Key Words

- **Hydration** — having enough water in your body
- **Dehydration** — not having enough water in your body
- **Thirst** — the feeling that tells you to drink water
- **Urine** — liquid waste from your body; its color shows your hydration
