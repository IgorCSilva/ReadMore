# Physical Exercise — Topic 2: Cardio Fundamentals

## What Is Cardio?

**Cardio** is exercise that makes your heart beat faster. It works your heart and lungs. Cardio helps you breathe better and feel stronger.

Cardio is also called **aerobic exercise**.

## Types of Cardio

You can choose many types of cardio. Here are some easy ones:

- Walking
- Running
- Riding a bike
- Swimming
- Dancing

Pick a type you enjoy. You will do it more often.

## How Hard Should You Work?

You don't need to work very hard. Use the **talk test** to check your effort.

- Too easy: You can sing while you move.
- Just right: You can talk, but you breathe harder.
- Too hard: You can't talk at all.

Try to stay in the just right zone most of the time.

## How Much Cardio Do You Need?

A good goal is 20 to 30 minutes of cardio, most days of the week.

You don't need to do it all at once. You can split it into smaller parts.

- 10 minutes in the morning
- 10 minutes in the afternoon
- 10 minutes in the evening

Any movement counts. Start with what you can do today.

## Practice: Your 20–30 Minute Beginner Cardio Session

Now it's your turn. Choose one cardio activity: walk, run, or bike.

**Step 1:** Choose your activity. Example: walking.

**Step 2:** Warm up for 5 minutes. Go slow.

**Step 3:** Do your activity for 15 to 20 minutes. Use the talk test to check your effort.

**Step 4:** Cool down for 5 minutes. Go slow again.

**Step 5:** Drink water after you finish.

### Sample Beginner Cardio Session

**Beginner Walk or Run Plan**

| Part | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Walk slow | 5 minutes |
| Main part | Walk or jog | 15–20 minutes |
| Cool-down | Walk slow | 5 minutes |

Total time: 25 to 30 minutes.

## Key Words

- **Cardio** — exercise that makes your heart beat faster
- **Aerobic exercise** — another name for cardio
- **Talk test** — a simple way to check how hard you are working
- **Warm-up** — slow movement before the main exercise
- **Cool-down** — slow movement after the main exercise
