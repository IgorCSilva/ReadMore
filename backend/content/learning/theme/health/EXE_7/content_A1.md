# Physical Exercise — Topic 7: Recovery & Injury Prevention

## Why Rest Days Matter

Exercise is good for your body. But your body also needs rest.

When you exercise, your muscles get small tears. Rest lets your muscles repair. This is how your muscles get stronger.

If you never rest, your body cannot repair itself. This can cause:

- Constant tiredness
- Weak performance
- Injuries

A **rest day** is a day with no hard exercise. Most people need one or two rest days every week.

## Warm-Up and Cool-Down

A **warm-up** is light movement before you exercise. It wakes up your muscles and raises your heart rate slowly.

A good warm-up takes 5 to 10 minutes. It can include:

- Light walking or jogging
- Arm circles
- Leg swings
- Slow bodyweight squats

A **cool-down** is light movement after you exercise. It helps your heart rate go back to normal slowly.

A good cool-down takes 5 to 10 minutes. It can include:

- Slow walking
- Gentle stretching
- Deep breathing

Skipping the warm-up or cool-down raises your risk of injury.

## Recognizing Overtraining and Early Injury Signs

**Overtraining** happens when you exercise too much without enough rest.

Watch for these signs of overtraining:

- You feel tired all the time
- You sleep badly
- Your performance gets worse, not better
- Small aches do not go away

Also watch for early signs of injury:

- Sharp pain during movement
- Pain that continues after you stop
- Swelling in a joint or muscle
- A joint feels unstable

If you notice these signs, rest. Do not push through sharp pain.

## Practice: Warm-Up and Cool-Down Routine Template

Now it's your turn. Use this simple template before and after your next workout.

**Step 1:** Before your workout, do 5 minutes of light movement to raise your heart rate.

**Step 2:** Do 3 or 4 dynamic movements from the warm-up list below.

**Step 3:** Start your workout.

**Step 4:** After your workout, walk slowly for 2 or 3 minutes.

**Step 5:** Do 3 or 4 stretches from the cool-down list below. Hold each stretch for 20 to 30 seconds.

### Warm-Up and Cool-Down Routine

| Phase | Movement | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Light jogging or marching in place | 2 minutes |
| Warm-up | Arm circles | 30 seconds each direction |
| Warm-up | Leg swings | 30 seconds each leg |
| Warm-up | Slow bodyweight squats | 1 minute |
| Cool-down | Slow walking | 2 minutes |
| Cool-down | Hamstring stretch | 20 to 30 seconds each leg |
| Cool-down | Chest stretch | 20 to 30 seconds |
| Cool-down | Deep breathing | 1 minute |

Do this routine every time you exercise, not only on hard days.

## Key Words

- **Rest day** — a day with no hard exercise
- **Warm-up** — light movement before you exercise
- **Cool-down** — light movement after you exercise
- **Overtraining** — exercising too much without enough rest
- **Injury** — damage to your body from exercise or movement
