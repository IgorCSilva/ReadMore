# Physical Exercise — Topic 6: Exercise for Desk Workers & Sedentary Lifestyles

## Why Sitting Too Long Is a Problem

Many people sit for many hours every day. They sit at a desk, in a car, or on a couch.

Sitting too long is not good for your body. It can cause:

- Back pain
- Neck pain
- Stiff shoulders
- Low energy

Your body needs to move, even during a busy workday. Small movements help a lot.

## Good Posture at Your Desk

**Posture** is how you hold your body. Good posture protects your back and neck.

Simple posture tips:

- Keep your feet flat on the floor
- Keep your back straight, not curved
- Keep your shoulders relaxed, not raised
- Keep your screen at eye level

Check your posture a few times every hour. Fix it if you notice you are slouching.

## Micro-Breaks

A **micro-break** is a very short break, just one or two minutes. You take a micro-break often during the day, not only once.

Micro-breaks help your body in simple ways:

- They rest your eyes
- They reduce stiffness
- They improve your focus after you sit again

A good rule: stand up and move for one or two minutes every 30 to 60 minutes.

## Practice: Desk-Friendly Stretches and Movement Breaks

Now it's your turn. Use this simple routine during your workday.

**Step 1:** Set a reminder every hour.

**Step 2:** When the reminder rings, stand up from your chair.

**Step 3:** Do two or three stretches from the list below.

**Step 4:** Walk a little, even just to the kitchen or the window.

**Step 5:** Sit back down with good posture.

### Desk-Friendly Stretch Routine

| Stretch | How to Do It | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Neck roll | Slowly roll your head in a circle | 30 seconds |
| Shoulder roll | Roll your shoulders back, then forward | 30 seconds |
| Seated twist | Sit straight, turn your upper body left, then right | 20 seconds each side |
| Wrist stretch | Extend one arm, pull your fingers back gently | 15 seconds each hand |
| Standing stretch | Stand up, reach your arms up high | 20 seconds |

Do this short routine two or three times during your workday.

## Key Words

- **Posture** — how you hold your body
- **Micro-break** — a very short break, one or two minutes
- **Stiffness** — feeling tight or sore in your muscles or joints
- **Sedentary** — sitting most of the time, without much movement
- **Stretch** — a movement that makes a muscle longer and more relaxed
