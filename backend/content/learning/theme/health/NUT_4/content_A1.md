# Nutrition — Topic 4: Meal Planning & Portion Control

## What Is Meal Planning?

Meal planning means you decide your meals before you eat them. You choose breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the whole week, not just for one day.

**Meal planning** helps you eat better and spend less money.

## Why Plan Your Meals?

Planning your meals has many benefits:

- You save time during the week
- You spend less money at the store
- You eat more healthy food
- You make fewer last-minute choices, like fast food

When you don't plan, you often choose fast food or snacks. These choices are not always healthy.

## Reading Hunger and Fullness Cues

Your body sends signals when you are hungry or full. Learning to read these signals helps you eat the right amount.

Use this simple **hunger scale** from 1 to 10:

- Level 1 or 2: Very hungry, maybe dizzy or weak
- Level 3 or 4: Hungry, your stomach feels empty
- Level 5 or 6: Comfortable, not hungry and not full
- Level 7 or 8: Full, satisfied
- Level 9 or 10: Very full, uncomfortable

Try to eat when you are at level 3 or 4. Stop eating when you reach level 7 or 8. Eat slowly. It takes time for your brain to feel **fullness**.

## Portion-Size Reference Tricks

You don't always need a scale to measure your food. You can use your hand as a simple guide.

| Part of Your Hand | Food Type | Portion |
|---|---|---|
| Palm | Protein (chicken, fish, meat) | One palm size |
| Fist | Vegetables | One fist size |
| Cupped hand | Carbs (rice, pasta, potato) | One cupped hand |
| Thumb | Fat (oil, butter, nuts) | One thumb size |

This is called the **hand-portion method**. It works for almost everyone because your hand grows with your body.

## Key Words

- **Meal planning** — deciding your meals before you eat them
- **Portion control** — eating the right amount of food
- **Hunger scale** — a way to measure how hungry or full you feel
- **Fullness** — the feeling of having eaten enough
- **Hand-portion method** — using your hand to measure food portions
