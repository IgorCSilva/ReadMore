# Physical Exercise — Topic 5: Building a Sustainable Routine

## What Is a Sustainable Routine?

A **routine** is a plan you repeat every week. A **sustainable** routine is a routine you can keep for a long time, not just one week.

A good routine mixes different types of exercise:

- Cardio
- Strength training
- Mobility

Each type helps your body in a different way. A good week has some of all three.

## Why Combine Cardio, Strength, and Mobility?

Cardio makes your heart strong. Strength training makes your muscles strong. Mobility keeps your joints healthy.

If you do only one type every day, your body gets tired in the same way every time. This can lead to **burnout**.

Burnout happens when you exercise too much, without enough rest. You feel tired, sore, and you lose motivation.

Mixing different types of exercise across the week helps you avoid burnout. It also makes exercise more fun.

## How Many Days Should You Exercise?

You don't need to exercise every day. A good starting point is:

- 3 to 5 active days per week
- At least 1 full rest day

A **rest day** is a day with no hard exercise. Rest days help your muscles repair and grow stronger.

Listen to your body. If you feel very tired or sore, take an extra rest day.

## Building Your Weekly Schedule

A simple way to build a schedule is to give each active day a main focus:

- Some days for cardio
- Some days for strength
- Some days for mobility
- One or two days for rest

You can also combine two types in one day, like a short cardio warm-up before strength training.

## Practice: Your Sample Weekly Workout Schedule

Now it's your turn. Build a simple weekly schedule you can actually follow.

**Step 1:** Choose how many active days you want this week (3 to 5 is a good start).

**Step 2:** Give each active day one main focus: cardio, strength, or mobility.

**Step 3:** Add at least one full rest day.

**Step 4:** Write your schedule on paper or in your phone, so you remember it.

**Step 5:** Follow the schedule for one week. Then adjust it if something feels too hard or too easy.

### Sample Weekly Schedule

**Beginner Weekly Routine**

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Cardio |
| Tuesday | Strength training |
| Wednesday | Mobility |
| Thursday | Rest |
| Friday | Cardio |
| Saturday | Strength training |
| Sunday | Rest |

This schedule has 4 active days, 2 strength days, 1 mobility day, and 2 rest days.

## Key Words

- **Routine** — a plan you repeat every week
- **Sustainable** — something you can keep doing for a long time
- **Burnout** — feeling very tired and losing motivation from too much exercise
- **Rest day** — a day with no hard exercise, used to recover
- **Schedule** — a plan that says what you do on each day
