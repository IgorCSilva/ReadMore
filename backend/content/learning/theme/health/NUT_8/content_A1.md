# Nutrition — Topic 8: Mindful Eating

## What Is Mindful Eating?

**Mindful eating** means you pay full attention while you eat. You notice the taste, the smell, and the texture of your food. You eat slower. You notice when your body feels hungry and when it feels full.

Many people eat fast, in front of a screen, or while thinking about other things. This is the opposite of mindful eating. It is easy to eat too much when you are not paying attention.

## Physical Hunger or Emotional Hunger

**Physical hunger** comes from your body. It grows slowly. Your stomach feels empty. Any food sounds good.

**Emotional eating** comes from your feelings, not your stomach. It can come from stress, sadness, or boredom. It often wants one specific food, like chips or ice cream. It can happen even right after a meal.

Before you eat, ask yourself: "Is my body hungry, or are my feelings hungry?" This simple question helps you choose better.

## Eating Without Distraction

Eating in front of a phone or a TV makes it hard to notice your hunger and fullness signals. You keep eating after your body is already full, because your mind is somewhere else.

Try to eat at a table, without a screen, at least once a day. Put your fork down between bites. Chew slowly.

## Listening to Fullness

Your stomach sends a "full" signal to your brain, but this signal takes about 20 minutes to arrive. If you eat too fast, you can eat past the point of fullness before you feel it.

A simple trick: stop eating when you feel comfortably satisfied, not completely full. Wait a few minutes. If you are still hungry, you can eat more.

## Practice: A Mindful-Eating Exercise for Your Next Meal

Try this simple exercise the next time you eat a meal:

1. Before you start, take one slow breath. Look at your food.
2. Rate your hunger from 1 (not hungry) to 10 (very hungry).
3. Put away your phone. Turn off the TV.
4. Take small bites. Chew slowly, at least 15 times per bite.
5. Put your fork down between bites.
6. Halfway through the meal, stop. Rate your fullness from 1 to 10.
7. Finish eating only if you are still truly hungry.

Do this exercise one time today. Notice how it feels different from your normal meals.

## Key Words

- **Mindful eating** — paying full attention to taste, smell, texture, hunger, and fullness while eating
- **Physical hunger** — hunger that comes from your body and grows slowly
- **Emotional eating** — eating because of feelings like stress or boredom, not real body hunger
- **Distraction** — something that takes your attention away from your food, like a phone or TV
- **Fullness** — the feeling of having eaten enough
