# Physical Exercise — Topic 4: Flexibility & Mobility

## What Is Mobility?

**Mobility** is how well your joints can move. **Flexibility** is how well your muscles can stretch.

Both help your body move freely, without pain or stiffness.

Mobility and flexibility help you:

- Bend down easily
- Reach overhead without pain
- Turn and twist your body
- Move better in daily life

## Why Does Mobility Work Matter?

Many people only think about cardio and strength. They forget about mobility.

Without mobility work, your body can become stiff. A stiff body can lead to:

- Pain in the back, neck, or knees
- A higher risk of injury
- Trouble with simple movements, like tying your shoes

A few minutes of mobility work every day keeps your joints healthy.

## Stretching: Two Types

There are two main types of stretching.

- **Dynamic stretching** — you move while you stretch. Example: arm circles, leg swings. Good before exercise.
- **Static stretching** — you hold a stretch still. Example: touching your toes and holding it. Good after exercise, or any time of day.

Use dynamic stretching to warm up. Use static stretching to cool down or relax.

## When Should You Stretch?

You can stretch any time, but two moments work very well:

- In the morning, to wake up your body
- After exercise, when your muscles are warm

Hold each static stretch for 20 to 30 seconds. Don't bounce. Breathe slowly.

## Practice: Your 10-Minute Daily Stretching Routine

Now it's your turn. This routine takes about 10 minutes. Do it every day, or at least a few times a week.

**Step 1:** Find a quiet space with room to move.

**Step 2:** Do each stretch below. Hold each one for 20 to 30 seconds on each side (if it has a side).

**Step 3:** Breathe slowly and deeply during each stretch.

**Step 4:** Don't force a stretch. A little pull is fine. Pain is not fine.

**Step 5:** Repeat the whole routine once if you have extra time.

### Sample Daily Stretching Routine

**Beginner Mobility Routine**

| Stretch | Area | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Neck tilt (side to side) | Neck | 20 seconds each side |
| Shoulder rolls | Shoulders | 10 rolls each way |
| Standing toe touch | Back and legs | 30 seconds |
| Hip circles | Hips | 10 circles each way |
| Quad stretch (standing) | Front of leg | 20 seconds each side |
| Ankle circles | Ankles | 10 circles each way |

Total time: about 10 minutes.

## Key Words

- **Mobility** — how well your joints can move
- **Flexibility** — how well your muscles can stretch
- **Dynamic stretching** — stretching while moving, good before exercise
- **Static stretching** — holding a stretch still, good after exercise
- **Stiffness** — when your body feels tight and doesn't move easily
