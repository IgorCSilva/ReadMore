# Physical Exercise — Topic 1: Benefits of Exercise & Getting Started

## Why Move Your Body?

Your body needs movement every day. Movement for your health is called **exercise**. Exercise helps your body and your mind.

Exercise gives you:

- More energy
- A strong heart
- Strong muscles and bones
- Better sleep
- Less stress

You don't need a gym. You don't need special equipment. You just need to start.

## Why Is It Hard to Start?

Many people want to exercise. But they don't start. This is normal.

Common reasons people don't start:

- "I don't have time."
- "I'm too tired."
- "I don't know what to do."
- "I don't like exercise."

These reasons are real. But small steps can fix them.

## Start Small

You don't need a long workout. A short walk is a good start.

Try this rule: **something is better than nothing**.

- 5 minutes is better than 0 minutes.
- A short walk is better than no walk.
- One day of movement is better than no days.

## Set a Realistic Goal

A **realistic goal** is a goal you can really do. Big goals can feel too hard. Small goals help you start and continue.

Bad goal: "I will run 10 kilometers every day."

Good goal: "I will walk for 10 minutes, three days this week."

Use this simple plan:

| Question | Your Answer |
|---|---|
| What will you do? | Example: walk |
| How long? | Example: 10 minutes |
| How many days? | Example: 3 days |
| When? | Example: after lunch |

## Practice: Your First-Week Starter Routine

Now it's your turn. Use this simple **routine** for your first week.

**Step 1:** Choose one easy activity. Example: walking.

**Step 2:** Choose a short time. Example: 10 minutes.

**Step 3:** Choose three days this week. Example: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.

**Step 4:** Write your plan:

| Day | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Walk | 10 minutes |
| Wednesday | Walk | 10 minutes |
| Friday | Walk | 10 minutes |

**Step 5:** After each day, mark it as done.

### Sample First-Week Routine

**Beginner Walking Plan**

- Day 1: Walk for 10 minutes. Go at an easy speed.
- Day 2: Rest day. Stretch your legs for 5 minutes.
- Day 3: Walk for 10 minutes. Go a little faster.
- Day 4: Rest day.
- Day 5: Walk for 15 minutes.
- Day 6: Rest day.
- Day 7: Walk for 15 minutes. Feel proud of your first week!

## Key Words

- **Exercise** — movement that is good for your body and mind
- **Realistic goal** — a goal you can really do
- **Routine** — a plan you repeat regularly
- **Rest day** — a day without hard exercise, for recovery
- **Starter routine** — a simple plan to begin a new habit
