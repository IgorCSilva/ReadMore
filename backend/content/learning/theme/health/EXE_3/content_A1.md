# Physical Exercise — Topic 3: Strength Training Basics

## What Is Strength Training?

**Strength training** is exercise that makes your muscles stronger. You can use your own body weight, or you can use **dumbbells** or other weights.

Strength training helps you:

- Build strong muscles
- Protect your bones
- Move better every day
- Burn more energy, even at rest

You don't need a gym. You don't need heavy weights to start.

## Why Does It Matter at Any Age?

Many people think strength training is only for young people or athletes. This is not true.

As you get older, your body can lose muscle. Strength training helps you:

- Stay strong
- Keep your balance
- Carry bags, climb stairs, and stand up easily

Strength training is good for everyone, at every age.

## Basic Movement Patterns

Most strength exercises use four basic movement patterns. Learn these four, and you can do many exercises.

- **Push** — you push something away from your body. Example: a push-up.
- **Pull** — you pull something toward your body. Example: a row.
- **Squat** — you bend your knees and hips, like sitting in a chair. Example: a bodyweight squat.
- **Hinge** — you bend at your hips, with your back straight. Example: a hip hinge or a deadlift.

## How Often Should You Train?

You don't need to train every day. Your muscles need time to rest and grow.

A good goal is 2 to 3 strength sessions per week, with a rest day between sessions.

## Practice: Your Full-Body Strength Routine

Now it's your turn. Use this simple routine. You only need your body weight, or light dumbbells if you have them.

**Step 1:** Choose a quiet space. You need room to move your arms and legs.

**Step 2:** Do a short warm-up. Walk or move your arms for 3 minutes.

**Step 3:** Do each exercise below. Do 10 repetitions of each one.

**Step 4:** Rest for 1 minute between exercises.

**Step 5:** Repeat the whole routine two times.

### Sample Full-Body Routine

**Beginner Bodyweight Routine**

| Exercise | Pattern | Repetitions |
|---|---|---|
| Push-up (or knee push-up) | Push | 10 |
| Bent-over row (with a bag or light dumbbells) | Pull | 10 |
| Bodyweight squat | Squat | 10 |
| Hip hinge | Hinge | 10 |
| Plank | Core | 20 seconds |

Rest for 1 minute. Then repeat the routine one more time.

## Key Words

- **Strength training** — exercise that makes your muscles stronger
- **Dumbbell** — a small, heavy weight you hold in your hand
- **Push** — a movement that pushes something away from your body
- **Pull** — a movement that pulls something toward your body
- **Hinge** — a movement that bends at the hips, with a straight back
