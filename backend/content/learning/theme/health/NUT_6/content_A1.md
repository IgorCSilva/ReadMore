# Nutrition — Topic 6: Reducing Sugar & Processed Foods

## What Is a Processed Food?

Food changes when people make it in a factory. This is **processed food**. Not all processed food is bad. But some processed food has too much sugar, salt, and fat.

## What Is Added Sugar?

**Added sugar** is sugar that is not naturally in the food. Companies put it in food and drinks to make them taste sweet. Added sugar is in soda, candy, cookies, and even bread.

Natural sugar is different. Fruit has natural sugar. Milk has natural sugar too. This sugar comes with other good things, like fiber and vitamins.

## What Is Ultra-Processed Food?

**Ultra-processed food** has many ingredients you cannot find in a home kitchen. Look at the ingredient list. If it has many strange names, it is probably ultra-processed.

Examples of ultra-processed food:

- Packaged cookies and cakes
- Soda and sugary drinks
- Instant noodles
- Chips and salty snacks
- Candy

## Why Too Much Sugar and Processed Food Is a Problem

Too much added sugar can cause weight gain. It can also raise your risk of diabetes and heart problems. Ultra-processed food often has little fiber and few vitamins. You feel hungry again soon after eating it.

## The Whole-Food Alternative

A **whole food** is close to its natural form. An apple is a whole food. Apple juice with added sugar is not. Plain rice is a whole food. A flavored instant rice mix is not.

Eating more whole food and less processed food is a simple, powerful change.

## Practice: 5 Easy Whole-Food Swaps

Try these **swaps** this week. Each one trades a processed food for a whole-food option.

**Swap 1:** Change soda for water with fresh lemon or mint.

**Swap 2:** Change packaged cookies for a piece of fruit or a handful of nuts.

**Swap 3:** Change flavored instant oatmeal for plain oats with fresh fruit and a little honey.

**Swap 4:** Change chips for air-popped popcorn or raw vegetables with hummus.

**Swap 5:** Change sugary breakfast cereal for plain yogurt with fruit.

Pick one swap today. Try it for one week before you add another.

## Key Words

- **Processed food** — food changed from its natural state, often in a factory
- **Added sugar** — sugar put into food or drinks that is not naturally there
- **Ultra-processed food** — food with many industrial ingredients, far from its natural form
- **Whole food** — food close to its natural, unprocessed form
- **Swap** — trading one food choice for a healthier one
